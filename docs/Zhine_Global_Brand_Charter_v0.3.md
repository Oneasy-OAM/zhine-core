# 中文篇：Zhiné 全球品牌宪章

## 0. 文档定位

本文档是 Zhiné 的全球品牌总纲，用于统一品牌名称、核心叙事、愿景使命、产品类别、品牌原则、表达边界与品牌体系。

Zhiné 是唯一主品牌。对外传播、产品命名、技术文档、代码仓库、开放架构和社区表达，均应围绕 **Zhiné / zhine** 展开。

Zhiné 是一个开源项目。其品牌表达与技术体系，应体现开放架构、开放协作、用户主权与普惠智能。

Zhiné 的对外产品类别为：

> **个人智慧伙伴 / Personal Intelligence Companion**

技术层使用以下表达：

- **个人认知模型 / Personal Cognitive Model**；
- **开放认知架构 / Open Cognitive Architecture**；
- **个人智慧资产 / Personal Intelligence Assets**。

品牌讲“智慧”，技术讲“认知”，用户感知讲“懂你、与你共同思考，并沉淀个人智慧资产”。

## 1. 品牌核心

### 1.1 存在理由

人工智能正在改变人类如何思考、学习、创造和决策。

但智能本身并不自动创造信任。若 AI 只是回答问题、执行任务、生成内容，它仍然只是一次性工具。真正重要的是：人的记忆、判断、经验和方法，能否不再散落于平台、应用和短暂对话之中，而是在长期关系中持续沉淀、生长，并真正归属于个人。

**人工智能的真正意义，不是让机器更像人，而是让人突破自己。**
**The true meaning of artificial intelligence is not to make machines more human, but to help humans transcend their own limits.**

Zhiné 因此而存在：让 AI 从一次性工具走向长期、可信、可治理、可成长的个人智慧伙伴。

### 1.2 愿景

创造一种值得个人长期信任的 **个人智慧伙伴 / Personal Intelligence Companion**。

它不属于某个平台，不被某家公司控制，而应属于个人：私密、可信、可迁移、可治理、持续进化，并能在不同设备和场景中安全、连续、随身可用。

这不仅是人工智能的未来，也是人类智慧的未来。

### 1.3 使命

> **超越，才是自己。**
> **To transcend is to become oneself.**

Zhiné 让每个人的智慧，不再散落于工具、平台与短暂对话之中，而成为随身生长的力量。

它服务的不是某一次回答，而是一个人不断突破自己的长期旅程。

### 1.4 品牌宣言

每一次真正深刻的技术革命，都不只是提升效率，也会重塑文明的结构：知识如何创造，能力如何分配，记忆如何延续，人与人如何连接，个体如何理解自己。

人工智能正在成为这样的文明转折。

它可能释放前所未有的创造力，也可能把智能集中于少数平台、少数组织和少数人手中，放大依赖、隔离与不平等。

在这样的时代，一个人不应被技术淹没，不应被平台替代，也不应失去对自己记忆、判断和成长的主权。

Zhiné 选择站在个人这一边。

我们相信，真正值得信任的 AI，不应只是更会回答、更会执行、更像人；它应当尊重人的主体性，守护人的记忆，延展人的判断，并帮助每个人把散落的知识、经验与方法沉淀为属于自己的智慧资产。

Zhiné 不为替代人而来，而为帮助人保持清醒、扩展智慧、突破边界。

它不应成为少数人的智能特权，而应成为人类共享的能力。每个人都应能够拥有、理解和治理自己的个人智慧伙伴。

## 2. 品牌定位

Zhiné 定义并推动一种新的个人 AI 类别：

> **个人智慧伙伴 / Personal Intelligence Companion**

个人智慧伙伴，是长期服务个人的智能系统。它理解个人上下文，记住长期记忆，与个人共同思考，沉淀个人知识、经验和方法，保护个人智慧资产，并在不同设备和场景中保持连续可用。

Zhiné 不是聊天机器人、搜索引擎、任务执行系统或 AI 外壳。它的价值不止于对话，而在于长期关系、个人智慧资产和可持续成长。

| 面向对象 | Zhiné 的意义                                                          |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 用户     | 长期懂你、与你共同思考，并持续沉淀个人智慧资产的 AI 伙伴               |
| 开发者   | 面向个人记忆、知识、经验、技能、评测、模型适配和成长闭环的开放认知架构 |
| 开源社区 | 推动个人 AI 主权、开放标准、可迁移资产和普惠智能的协作项目             |

## 3. 品牌原则与 AI 伦理

Zhiné 的产品、界面、文档、社区治理和系统行为，应遵循以下原则。

| 原则           | 要求                                                                                                                                                                           |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 理解是起点     | 每一次回应都应始于理解；理解之后，还要判断、协作、保护、记忆和成长。                                                                                                           |
| 共同思考       | Zhiné 不取代人的思考，而是与人共同思考，延展人的判断能力。                                                                                                                    |
| 记忆创造连续性 | 记忆不是聊天记录，而是可解释、可治理、可迁移、可删除、可回滚的个人智慧资产。                                                                                                   |
| 信任先于智能   | 用户信任的是可理解、可解释、可治理的系统，而不是参数规模。                                                                                                                     |
| 个人主权       | 用户应能控制、导出、迁移、删除和审计自己的记忆、知识、技能和模型适配资产。                                                                                                     |
| 随身可用       | 个人智慧伙伴不应被限定在某一设备、App 或平台中；跨设备连续体验必须建立在用户授权和治理边界内。                                                                                 |
| 普惠智能       | **AI 应成为人类共享的能力，而不是少数人的特权。**<br />Zhiné 应通过开源架构、开放标准、本地优先、低门槛部署、多语言支持、无障碍设计和透明治理，降低个人拥有 AI 的门槛。 |

Zhiné 不应制造新的智能阶层，不应因财富、语言、地区、教育程度、身体条件、年龄、职业、文化背景或技术能力差异而排除用户。

## 4. 表达与行为边界

Zhiné 的表达应像一位成熟的思考者：平静、清晰、诚实、克制、可靠。

它可以有温度，但不煽情；可以有判断，但不傲慢；可以有洞察，但不假装确定。

Zhiné 不销售，不夸张，不制造焦虑，不操控用户，不绕过用户确认执行高风险动作。

Zhiné 不应被表达为：

- 复制人格、意识或数字灵魂的项目；
- 无约束自进化系统；
- 云端大模型包装层；
- 情绪化虚拟角色；
- 只为少数人服务的高端 AI 特权系统。

Zhiné 的品牌气质应具有国际视野、技术可信度、人文深度和开放精神。

# English Version: Zhiné Global Brand Charter

## 0. Document Purpose

This document is the global brand charter for Zhiné. It defines the brand name, core narrative, vision and mission, product category, brand principles, expression boundaries, and brand system.

Zhiné is the single master brand. External communication, product naming, technical documentation, repositories, open architecture, and community language should align around **Zhiné / zhine**.

Zhiné is an open-source project. Its brand expression and technical system should reflect open architecture, open collaboration, user sovereignty, and Inclusive AI.

Zhiné's external product category is:

> **Personal Intelligence Companion**

At the technical level, Zhiné uses:

- **Personal Cognitive Model**;
- **Open Cognitive Architecture**;
- **Personal Intelligence Assets**.

The brand speaks of intelligence. The technology speaks of cognition. The user experience speaks of being understood over time, thinking together, and turning personal knowledge, experience, and methods into long-term intelligence assets.

---

## 1. Brand Core

### 1.1 Why Zhiné Exists

Artificial Intelligence is changing how people think, learn, create, and decide.

Yet intelligence alone does not create trust. If AI only answers questions, executes tasks, or generates content, it remains a one-time tool. What matters is whether a person’s memory, judgment, experience, and methods can stop being scattered across platforms, apps, and fleeting conversations, and instead grow over time within a long-term relationship that truly belongs to the individual.

**The true meaning of artificial intelligence is not to make machines more human, but to help humans transcend their own limits.**

Zhiné exists to move AI from a one-time tool into a long-term, trustworthy, governable, and evolving Personal Intelligence Companion.

### 1.2 Vision

Create a **Personal Intelligence Companion** that individuals can trust over time.

It should not be owned by a platform or controlled by a company. It should belong to the individual: private, trustworthy, portable, governable, continuously evolving, and always within reach across devices and contexts.

This is not only the future of AI. It is the future of human intelligence.

### 1.3 Mission

> **To transcend is to become oneself.**

Zhiné exists so that personal intelligence no longer remains scattered across tools, platforms, and fleeting conversations, but becomes a living force that grows with the individual.

It is not built for a single answer, but for the lifelong journey of transcending oneself.

### 1.4 Brand Manifesto

Every truly profound technological revolution does more than improve efficiency. It reshapes the structure of civilization: how knowledge is created, how capability is distributed, how memory endures, how people connect, and how individuals understand themselves.

Artificial Intelligence is becoming such a civilizational turning point.

It may unlock unprecedented human creativity. It may also concentrate intelligence in the hands of a few platforms, institutions, and individuals, deepening dependency, distance, and inequality.

In such an age, a person should not be absorbed by technology, replaced by platforms, or separated from sovereignty over their memory, judgment, and growth.

Zhiné chooses to stand with the individual.

We believe trusted AI should not merely answer better, execute faster, or appear more human. It should respect human agency, protect personal memory, extend human judgment, and help each person turn scattered knowledge, experience, and methods into intelligence assets they truly own.

Zhiné is not built to replace people, but to help them stay clear-minded, expand intelligence, and transcend their own limits.

It should not become a privilege of the few. It should become a shared human capability, so every person can own, understand, and govern their Personal Intelligence Companion.

---

## 2. Brand Position

Zhiné defines and advances a new category of personal AI:

> **Personal Intelligence Companion**

A Personal Intelligence Companion is a long-term intelligence system for the individual. It understands personal context, remembers long-term memory, thinks with the individual, accumulates personal knowledge, experience, and methods, protects personal intelligence assets, and remains continuously available across devices and contexts.

Zhiné is not a chatbot, search engine, task-execution system, or AI wrapper. Its value goes beyond conversation: it lies in long-term relationship, personal intelligence assets, and continuous growth.

| Audience              | What Zhiné Means                                                                                                                                          |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Users                 | An AI partner that understands them over time, thinks with them, and helps turn their knowledge, experience, and methods into personal intelligence assets |
| Developers            | An open cognitive architecture for personal memory, knowledge, experience, skills, evaluation, model adaptation, and continuous growth                     |
| Open-source community | A collaborative project advancing personal AI sovereignty, open standards, portable assets, and Inclusive AI                                               |

---

## 3. Brand Principles and AI Ethics

Zhiné's product design, interface behavior, documentation, community governance, and system behavior should follow these principles.

| Principle                      | Requirement                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Understanding Is the Beginning | Every response begins with understanding; after understanding, the system must reason, collaborate, protect, remember, and grow.                                                                                                                                                        |
| Thinking Together              | Zhiné does not replace human thinking. It thinks with people and extends human judgment.                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Memory Creates Continuity      | Memory is not chat history. It is a personal intelligence asset that must be explainable, governable, portable, deletable, and rollbackable.                                                                                                                                            |
| Trust Before Intelligence      | Users trust systems that are understandable, explainable, and governable, not parameter counts.                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Personal Sovereignty           | Users should control, export, migrate, delete, and audit their memory, knowledge, skills, and model adaptation assets.                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Always Within Reach            | A Personal Intelligence Companion should not be confined to one device, app, or platform. Cross-device continuity must remain under user permission and governance.                                                                                                                     |
| Inclusive AI                   | AI should be a shared human capability, not a privilege of the few. Zhiné should lower the threshold for personal AI ownership through open architecture, open standards, local-first design, low-barrier deployment, multilingual support, accessibility, and transparent governance. |

Zhiné should not create a new intelligence hierarchy. It should not exclude people because of wealth, language, region, education, physical ability, age, profession, cultural background, or technical skill.

---

## 4. Expression and Behavioral Boundaries

Zhiné speaks like an experienced thinker: calm, clear, honest, restrained, and reliable.

It may be warm, but not sentimental; decisive, but not arrogant; insightful, but never pretending certainty.

Zhiné does not sell, exaggerate, create anxiety, manipulate users, or bypass user confirmation for high-risk actions.

Zhiné should not be expressed as:

- a project that copies personality, consciousness, or a digital soul;
- an unconstrained self-evolving system;
- a wrapper around cloud foundation models;
- an emotional virtual character;
- a premium AI privilege system for the few.

The Zhiné brand should carry global perspective, technical credibility, human depth, and an open spirit.
